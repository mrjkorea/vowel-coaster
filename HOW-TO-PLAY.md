# How to play Vowel Coaster

Same URL on phone, tablet, classroom PC, and laptop.

1. Tap **TAP TO RIDE**.
2. Listen. One baked vowel sound plays once.
3. When the letter signs appear, pick the track that matches the sound.
4. Right track = drop. Wrong track = stuck. Miss the split = fly off the middle.
5. Unlock order comes from the pack JSON (default A → E → I → U → O). Two tracks first, then three.

## Phone / tablet / touch

- Tap the left or right side of the ride (or the big letter buttons).
- Three tracks: left, middle, right thirds of the screen.
- Pause and camera buttons stay in the corner.

## Computer / classroom PC

- **1** or **←** left track
- **2** or **→** right track (two-track rides)
- Three tracks: **1** / **←** left, **2** / **↑** / **↓** middle, **3** / **→** right
- **Space** pause
- **V** chase camera or orbit camera

## Audio

Offline files only. No `/api/tts`. No browser `speechSynthesis`.
Sounds are listed in `packs/*.json` and live under `audio/`.
