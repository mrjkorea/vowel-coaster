HOW TO PLAY

Same game on phone, tablet, classroom PC, and laptop.

  1. Tap TAP TO RIDE.
  2. Listen. One baked vowel sound plays once.
  3. Pick the track whose letter matches the sound.
  4. Right track = drop. Wrong track = stuck. Miss the fork = fly off.
  5. Letters unlock from the pack JSON. Default ride: A, then E, I, U, O. Two tracks, then three.

Touch

  • Tap left or right (or the letter buttons).
  • Three tracks: tap left, middle, or right.

Keys

  • 1 or ← left. 2 or → right on two-track rides.
  • Three tracks: 1 / ← left, 2 / ↑ / ↓ middle, 3 / → right.
  • Space pauses. V switches chase / orbit camera.

Audio is offline mp3/wav from the JSON pack. No live TTS.
